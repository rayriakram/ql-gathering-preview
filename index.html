Welcome

A community for everyone who has been to MMI

Quantum Leap Gathering is the Dutch community for people who have attended MMI, the Millionaire Mind Intensive, including Quantum Leap graduates. If you have been to MMI, you are welcome, even if you have not done Quantum Leap yet.

We meet face to face to connect, share our businesses, practise short pitches, support one another and put Gotong Royong into action. WhatsApp helps us stay in touch, but it can never replace the friendships, trust, shared experiences and opportunities we create when we meet in person.

Gotong Royong. Many hands. One heart. One community. One purpose.

We don't just attend this community. We build it together.

QL Speed Connection

Inspired by the MMI experience, we challenge each other to introduce ourselves in just 40 seconds, meet different members and discover how we can support one another. Prepare a short pitch of maximum 80 words, answering four powerful questions, as we learnt during MMI:

  1. What do you offer?
  2. Why should people buy it?
  3. Why you?
  4. Why now?

No business? No problem. Share your expertise, passion, project or what you are looking for. The goal is to give people a reason to connect with you.

Our Code of Honour

How we build this community together

  1. Introduce yourselfA short video is encouraged. Feel free to share your LinkedIn, Instagram or website so we can get to know you and connect with you.
  2. Ask for help and offer supportGeneral requests can be shared in the group. Please continue individual conversations privately.
  3. No direct selling or promotional postsThis is a space for connection, contribution and mutual support.
  4. Keep messages relevant and to the pointSo important community information remains visible.
  5. Join our live gatherings whenever possibleThis is where we turn online connections into real relationships and Gotong Royong into action.
